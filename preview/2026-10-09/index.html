Message from Mark

Dear all,

In St Paul's Cathedral there is a monumental painting by Holman Hunt of Jesus as the light of the world, who says 'Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if anyone hears my voice, and open the door, I will come in and dine with them, and they with me.' That invitation is for each of us, but we must open the door and invite him in.

This week is our service of Holy Communion. We are continuing with this series in the Epistle to the Philippians, and we will meet some of the next generation of Christian leaders, reflect on the distinctive nature of leadership in the Christian tradition and what we should be looking for in our leaders. The reading will be Philippians 2:19-30.

You can find a service sheet attached.

With love and prayers,
Mark

Last Week's Sermon

▶  Watch the sermon on YouTube

Not far from here, over the mountains, is a dark sky zone. There, as Mark Fletcher says, the sky “is ablaze with stars.” In Philippians 2, Paul calls us to “shine like stars” too. We do this by taking our faith seriously, with daily prayer and Bible reading. But Mark’s most important point is simple: “you can’t do this alone.” It is God “who works in you to will and to act.”

St Peter's Life Group

The St Peter’s Lifegroup resumed its weekly gatherings on Zoom on Monday 7th September at 19.00 Swiss time.

We are a small group of Jesus followers trying to make sense of the Bible and life.

Each week we seek to discover the wisdom in Scripture that can shape us into a community that trusts God, lives justly and loves both God and neighbour.

We are planning a varied programme, so that in any month we might meditate on a Psalm, study a biblical theme, examine a character or one of the commandments. We would love you to join us.

So, if you are not on our weekly Lifegroup mailing list but would like to know more, please email me.

St Peter’s Walkers: Along the Sarine to the Cascade du Ramaclé

This week, on a stunning autumn day, nine intrepid St Peter’s walkers followed the Sarine to the Cascade du Ramaclé. It was a chance to try the new path, built this summer to replace the one washed away in the storms of November 2024. Along the way, everyone was reminded of the beauty all around us: the river, the soaring mountains and the colours of autumn. We were reminded, too, of the joy of fellowship and good conversation. Many thanks to Dorothy for organising the walk.

Photo: Guy Girardet

This is probably the last of our summer walks. Thank you to everyone who has organised or joined a walk this year. If there is a chance for another walk, or even a winter walk, we will let people know via WhatsApp.

Upcoming Events

Remembrance Sunday, 8 November

On 8th November, St Peter’s will observe Remembrance Sunday. We remember all who gave their lives for others in the two World Wars of the 20th century and in the conflicts of the 21st century up to the present day. As we remember, we also pray for peace and commit ourselves to work for a more peaceful world. Poppies will be available nearer the time and all proceeds go to the Royal British Legion.
