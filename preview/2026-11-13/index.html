Message from Mark Fletcher

Dear St Peters,

This week we reach the final chapter of the Epistle to the Philippians, but we end with one last sublime passage as Paul speaks of what it means to be content in any and all circumstances. There have been so many amazing lines – which has been your favourite?

This will be a service of Evening Prayer and the reading will be Philippians 4:10-23. You can find an order of service below.

I'll look forward to being together with you once again.

With love and prayers,
Mark

Last Week's Sermon

▶  Watch the sermon on YouTube

“If you are not defined by what you choose to remember, you will be defined by what you can’t forget.” Mark Fletcher said this on Remembrance Sunday, preaching on Philippians 3:12-21. Old hurts must be faced and then put aside. Mark said that “just because they are part of your story doesn’t mean it has to be the whole story.” Paul also calls us to choose better role models. And Mark reminded us that “your citizenship is in heaven.”

St Peter's Life Group

The St Peter’s Lifegroup is a small group of Jesus followers trying to make sense of the Bible and life.

We meet on Zoom on Mondays at 19.00 Swiss time.

Each week we seek to discover the wisdom in Scripture that can shape us into a community that trusts God, lives justly and loves both God and neighbour.

We are planning a varied programme, so that in any month we might meditate on a Psalm, study a biblical theme, examine a character or one of the commandments. We would love you to join us.

So, if you are not on our weekly Lifegroup mailing list but would like to know more, please email us.

Anglican Communion Fund (ACF) – supported by St Peter’s

This Advent, the Anglican Communion Fund is raising money for Anglican health work around the world. Its priority is the Ebola response of the Anglican Church of Congo. In eastern DR Congo, the Church has reached nearly 44,000 people with prevention messages. It has trained local leaders and volunteers, visited thousands of homes, provided handwashing stations and countered dangerous misinformation. Many remote communities are still not reached, and lack of funding is the main barrier.
