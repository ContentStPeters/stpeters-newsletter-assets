Message from Mark

Dear all,

This is such a beautiful time of the year as Autumn begins, and all around us we can see the Harvest being gathered in. This week we will be giving thanks for all those who work the land, and for the creation which provides so abundantly for our needs, and we will be remembering our responsibility as stewards of this good earth.

It will be a service of Harvest Evening Prayer, and we are continuing our series in the Prison Epistles reflecting further what it looks like to conduct ourselves in a manner worthy of the gospel of Christ. The reading will be Philippians 2:1-11.

With love and prayers,
Mark

Harvest Festival

On Sunday, 27 September, St Peter’s celebrates Harvest Festival.

Harvest Festival is a service of thanks to God for our food and for all that we receive. Anglican churches are often decorated with fruit, vegetables, flowers and wheatsheaves. In many churches, people also bring tins and packets of food, which are given to those in need.

The tradition is old. Before the Reformation, on 1 August, a loaf baked from the first wheat was presented in church. It was called the Lammas loaf, from the Old English for “loaf-mass”.

Harvest Festivals are not only an Anglican tradition. In Switzerland, Reformed churches hold a culte des récoltes in the autumn. It is very similar: the church is decorated with fruit and vegetables, and the food is sometimes given to a local charity. And the popular harvest hymn We plough the fields and scatter began as a German poem by Matthias Claudius.

“You crown the year with your bounty.” (Psalm 65:11)

Everyone is welcome. After the service, there will be a bring-and-share supper. Please bring a dish to share.

For information, contact Marilyn Dyrbus.

St Peter's Life Group

The St Peter’s Lifegroup resumed its weekly gatherings on Zoom on Monday 7th September at 19.00 Swiss time.

We are a small group of Jesus followers trying to make sense of the Bible and life.

Each week we seek to discover the wisdom in Scripture that can shape us into a community that trusts God, lives justly and loves both God and neighbour.

We are planning a varied programme, so that in any month we might meditate on a Psalm, study a biblical theme, examine a character or one of the commandments. We would love you to join us.

So, if you are not on our weekly Lifegroup mailing list but would like to know more, please contact me at timdbarlow@gmail.com.
