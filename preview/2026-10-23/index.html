Message from David Hall

Mary and I are very much looking forward to being with you all on the Sundays of 25th October and 1st November. It will be lovely to revisit Château-d’Oex — where my mother was brought up and I enjoyed so many childhood holidays. I will be ably assisted by my wife Mary on the cello and pianists David Heath and Sean Groombridge on these Sundays and very much hoping that the wonderful St Peter’s singers and players will boost our singing so that we will have a very encouraging time together.

If you were looking for a humorous title for the two Sunday talks (also celebrating bad English grammar) it could be “Great Christian stories wot you haven’t looked at for a long time”, as I will be preaching the lessons modern day adult Christian disciples can learn from the true Bible stories of David and Goliath (from 1 Samuel 17:4–11 and 24–49) on 25th October, and The Healing of Naaman (from 2 Kings 5:1–19) on 1 November. I am working on the basis that it might have been some years since you last looked at these great stories of (and this might be a more spiritual title:) God’s powerful deliverance.

St Peter’s Singers: members, and anyone who would like to join, are warmly invited to sing on both Sundays. Please come early for the rehearsal at 4.30, before the service.

Last Week's Sermon

▶  Watch the sermon on YouTube

“Don’t settle for that which does not satisfy.” That was Mark Fletcher’s challenge on Sunday. In Philippians 3, Paul looks back on a brilliant life. He had status, learning and a blameless record. Yet he says, “I regard them as rubbish, in order that I might gain Christ.” Mark asked what we are chasing in our own lives. Paul’s answer is to know Christ. His power can make us “a light in the darkness.”

St Peter's Life Group

The St Peter’s Lifegroup resumed its weekly gatherings on Zoom on Monday 7th September at 19.00 Swiss time.

We are a small group of Jesus followers trying to make sense of the Bible and life.

Each week we seek to discover the wisdom in Scripture that can shape us into a community that trusts God, lives justly and loves both God and neighbour.

We are planning a varied programme, so that in any month we might meditate on a Psalm, study a biblical theme, examine a character or one of the commandments. We would love you to join us.

So, if you are not on our weekly Lifegroup mailing list but would like to know more, please email us.

Upcoming Events

Autumn Drinks and a Discussion on Prayer, Friday 30 October

On Friday 30 October at 5 pm, the Revd. David Hall will host an autumn version of our Drinks in the Garden. We will start with drinks, followed by an informal Bible-based discussion on prayer. We hope there will be some live music too.

Please note: because of the season, this event will take place at St Peter’s, not at Chalet Notre Dame.

Remembrance Sunday, 8 November

On 8th November, St Peter’s will observe Remembrance Sunday. We remember all who gave their lives for others in the two World Wars of the 20th century and in the conflicts of the 21st century up to the present day. As we remember, we also pray for peace and commit ourselves to work for a more peaceful world. Poppies will be available nearer the time and all proceeds go to the Royal British Legion.
