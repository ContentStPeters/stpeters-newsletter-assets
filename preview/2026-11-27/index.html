Message from Mark Fletcher

Dear St Peter's,

This Sunday is the start of Advent. It is such a lovely season that often gets lost in the busyness of Christmas. It is a time of preparation, a fast before the feast where we pause to learn from those who hoped and waited faithfully for the Christ child, and we set our hope on all that God is doing and will do in our world.

It will be a special service of Advent Evening Prayer and our reading will be Isaiah 40:1-8 and 28-31. You can find an order of service below.

Also, I’m reading the new book by John Mark Comer, called ‘Into the quiet’. It is a really fitting book for Advent, drawing on ancient wisdom for a noisy world, to lead us into practices of stillness and silence in prayer. I’d highly recommend it. There is also a documentary to go alongside it, which could be used individually or in small groups.

I'll look forward to seeing you on Sunday evening.

With love and prayers,
Mark

Last Week's Sermon

▶  Watch the sermon on YouTube

Mark Fletcher called the opening of the Sermon on the Mount “the most important speech in history.” In the Beatitudes, Jesus sets out what his kingdom is like. The humble, the merciful and the peacemakers are blessed. “There is another kingdom at work in this world. And there is a better king.” So we have a choice to make, Mark said: “We need to choose who it is that we are serving day by day.”

St Peter's Life Group

The St Peter’s Lifegroup is a small group of Jesus followers trying to make sense of the Bible and life.

We meet on Zoom on Mondays at 19.00 Swiss time.

Each week we seek to discover the wisdom in Scripture that can shape us into a community that trusts God, lives justly and loves both God and neighbour.

We are planning a varied programme, so that in any month we might meditate on a Psalm, study a biblical theme, examine a character or one of the commandments. We would love you to join us.

So, if you are not on our weekly Lifegroup mailing list but would like to know more, please email us.
