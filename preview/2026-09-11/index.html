Message from Mark

Dear all,

As Autumn begins, in this season full of beauty, we turn our thoughts towards harvest time, in gratitude for this good creation which sustains all life, and pray for all of those who nurture and farm it.

This week is our service of Holy Communion, which is such a special service, and the journey from the pews up into the sanctuary, to share bread and then wine should be a little journey of humility, repentance and faith, and of the grace and mercy of God. Approach it solemnly, prayerfully and gratefully, and God will meet with us in the sacrament.

We’re continuing in our new series on the Prison letters of St Paul, called ‘Light in the Darkness’. The reading will be Philippians 1:12-18. You can find an order of service below.

I’ll look forward to seeing you on Sunday

With love and prayers,
Mark

News from Ken

For those of you who remember Ken Kvistad — part of the congregation here at St Peter’s for a good while, and now worshipping with All Saints in Vevey — he has asked that his news, and his warm regards, reach St Peter’s too. As he begins treatment for cancer he wrote to his congregation at All Saints, and their newsletter of 6 September carried his note. He would like you to read it:

I wanted to share with you all, as I am facing an important crossroads in life. Next week I will begin a course of ultraviolet phototherapy for cancer of the tongue. The prognosis is good, but it will require 6-8 weeks of daily treatment in La Chaux-de-Fonds.

No pity or sympathy, please, as we all share difficulties, and as Apostle Paul reminds us, our strength in God grows through our trials and weaknesses. My only prayer is that I follow God’s will and keep Jesus close at hand.

Ecclesiastes reminds us that God has made everything beautiful in its time and has put eternity in our hearts. And so I say with Paul: “I thank my God every time I remember you, for your heartfelt partnership in sharing the living, loving healing Jesus Christ as he walks along with us being confident of this, that he who began a good work in us will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus.”

Ken would be glad to hear from anyone who remembers him. For those who would like to get in touch to wish him well, he can be reached at kakjrkvistad@gmail.com.

St Peter's Life Group

The St Peter’s Lifegroup resumed its weekly gatherings on Zoom on Monday 7th September at 19.00 Swiss time.

We are a small group of Jesus followers trying to make sense of the Bible and life.

Each week we seek to discover the wisdom in Scripture that can shape us into a community that trusts God, lives justly and loves both God and neighbour.

We are planning a varied programme, so that in any month we might meditate on a Psalm, study a biblical theme, examine a character or one of the commandments. We would love you to join us.

So, if you are not on our weekly Lifegroup mailing list but would like to know more, please contact me at timdbarlow@gmail.com.
